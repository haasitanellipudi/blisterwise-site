For pharmacies

Built for the pack room.

Know who's due

One list of every pack coming up, with prep and billing dates worked out for weekly, two-week and monthly patients.

Fill and check with confidence

Scan each bottle to catch the wrong drug, strength or expiry. A second person checks it before it leaves.

Hand off, roll forward

Record pickup or delivery, and the next due date moves forward on its own. Every step is logged.

For patients and families

An app that explains the pack.

Patients and caregivers see what's coming and what's inside, without calling the pharmacy.

  • When the next pack is ready

    Pickup or delivery day, updated as the pharmacy fills and checks it.

  • What's in each pocket

    Tap a pocket to see each pill, what it looks like and what it's for.

  • Messages from the pharmacy

    Like a dose change or an update on a refill.
