How it works

One pack, four steps.

  1. 01 · DUE

    Know who's due

    Every patient's next pack, with prep dates worked out for weekly, two-week and monthly cycles. Start a new patient from a photo of their grid: staff check each line before it's saved.

  2. 02 · FILL

    Fill by scanning

    Scan each bottle. Blisterwise checks it against the patient's medication list, stops the wrong drug, strength or form, and records the lot and expiry.

  3. 03 · CHECK

    A second person checks

    A pharmacist checks the pack with their own PIN. If they made it themselves, they have to give a reason, and it's logged.

  4. 04 · HAND OFF

    Hand off and roll forward

    Record pickup or delivery. The next due date moves forward on its own, and every step is logged.
