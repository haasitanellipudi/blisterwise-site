Privacy

Last updated November 15, 2026

This page explains what happens to the information you send us when you ask for a Blisterwise free trial on this website.

What we collect

Only what you type into the free trial form: your name, pharmacy name, work email, city and province, and anything you add in the optional note.

The form asks you not to include any patient information. Please don't.

Why we collect it

To contact you about your free trial, set it up, and understand what pharmacies need from Blisterwise. We don't use it for anything else.

Where it's kept

Trial requests are stored in Blisterwise's Google Workspace account (Google Sheets and Gmail). Google may store this information on servers outside Canada. Only Blisterwise can see it.

Who we share it with

No one. We don't sell, rent or share your information.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it for your trial. If you don't go on to use Blisterwise, we delete your request within 12 months.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing haasita@blisterwise.ca.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your browser's IP address when the page loads.

Questions

Email haasita@blisterwise.ca.
