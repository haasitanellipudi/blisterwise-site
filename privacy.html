Privacy

Last updated November 13, 2026

This page explains what happens to the information you send us when you apply for the Blisterwise beta on this website.

What we collect

Only what you type into the beta application form: your name, pharmacy name, role, city and province, work email, phone number (if you give it), and your answers about how your pharmacy handles compliance packs.

The form asks you not to include any patient information. Please don't.

Why we collect it

To contact you about the beta, to choose the beta pharmacies, and to understand what pharmacies need from Blisterwise. We don't use it for anything else.

Where it's kept

Applications are stored in Blisterwise's Google Workspace account (Google Sheets and Gmail). Google may store this information on servers outside Canada. Only Blisterwise can see it.

Who we share it with

No one. We don't sell, rent or share your information.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it for the beta. If you don't go on to use Blisterwise, we delete your application within 12 months.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing haasita@blisterwise.ca.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your browser's IP address when the page loads.

Questions

Email haasita@blisterwise.ca.
